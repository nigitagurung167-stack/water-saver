High Priority

Running Toilet Noise

Problem: Phantom flushing or continuous tank hiss

Root Cause Worn-out flapper seal or misadjusted fill valve floating mechanism.
Smart Solution Drop a few drops of food dye in tank. If color leaks into bowl without flushing, replace $8 flapper valve.
Up to 200 gal/day
Save ~$180/yr
Daily Waste

Waiting for Hot Water

Problem: Running tap for 2+ minutes to get warm

Root Cause Water sitting in long pipe runs cools down between uses.
Smart Solution Install an under-sink hot water recirculation pump or use a simple thermal pitcher to catch clean cold water for plants.
Up to 12 gal/day
Save ~$85/yr
Easy DIY

Weak Shower Pressure

Problem: High water consumption with unsatisfying flow

Root Cause Outdated 2.5+ GPM showerheads or mineral scale clogging nozzles.
Smart Solution Swap to an air-injecting High-Efficiency 1.75 GPM showerhead. Increases felt pressure while using 30% less water.
~2,900 gal/yr
Save ~$110/yr
Outdoor

Overwatered Lawn

Problem: High outdoor water bill & soggy turf

Root Cause Watering during midday heat (evaporation) or fixed timers running in rain.
Smart Solution Install a smart WiFi sprinkler controller ($60) that automatically pauses irrigation based on live rain forecasts.
~7,500 gal/yr
Save ~$220/yr
Common Fix

Steady Faucet Drip

Problem: Persistent drip-drip sound day & night

Root Cause Degraded rubber washer or worn internal ceramic cartridge.
Smart Solution Screw off faucet aerator and replace internal O-ring seal or add an ultra-low flow swivel aerator ($5).
~1,500 gal/yr
Save ~$45/yr
Behavior Fix

Handwashing Dish Waste

Problem: Leaving tap running while scrubbing dishes

Root Cause Continuous open stream averaging 2.2 gallons per minute.
Smart Solution Fill one side of sink with soapy water for scrubbing, or use EnergyStar dishwasher (uses 3.5 gal vs 20 gal by hand).
~5,000 gal/yr
Save ~$140/yr
